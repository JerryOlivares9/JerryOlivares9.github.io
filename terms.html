EDITH Mail is the Gmail integration of EDITH, privately operated personal-assistant software. This website explains the project; it does not provide public access to the assistant or offer a subscription.

Authorized access

Connected accounts must be owned or legitimately controlled by the person authorizing access. Gmail access is limited to the permissions granted through Google and the profile enabled by EDITH’s operator. Visiting this website does not connect a Google account.

Assistant output and actions

AI-generated answers and interpretations can be incomplete or incorrect. Check original messages and confirmed action results when details matter. Feature availability depends on the enabled tools and integrations. The current Gmail connection provides read access; other actions require their own authorization.

Privacy and access removal

The privacy policy explains data access, cloud processing and retention. Google authorization can be removed through Google Account connections. Existing saved records may require separate deletion.
