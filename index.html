What EDITH does

Useful help,
with context.

01

Find and understand email

Search an authorized Gmail account, read relevant messages, summarize conversations, and extract details from supported attachments.

02

Help with everyday tasks

Answer questions and use enabled tools, including reminders. An email’s details can help with a separately authorized task.

03

Keep the owner informed

Incoming-email screening and proactive notices are being tested. When enabled, these features assess new messages and may suggest or carry out permitted reminder actions before reporting the result.

The Gmail connection

Read access. Clear purpose.

EDITH requests Google’s gmail.readonly permission so it can search and read the connected mailbox and supported attachments. This connection cannot send email, create drafts, mark messages as read, move messages, or delete them.

Local models process some work on the owner’s computer. Relevant email content and attachment material can also be sent to OpenAI’s API to interpret information and produce answers. The privacy policy explains these data flows and the records EDITH retains.

Connecting Gmail requires the account owner’s Google authorization. Access can be removed in Google Account connections.
